Privacy Policy for Productivity and Wellness Assistant

At Productivity and Wellness Assistant, we respect your privacy and take it seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Data Collection and Use

We do not collect, access, store, track, or share any personal, identifiable, or sensitive information about you. All information related to the usage of this extension, including your preferences or settings, is stored locally in your browser’s storage and is not transmitted to any external servers.

Permissions

To provide its functionality, this extension requests the following permissions:

  • Notifications: This is required to send reminders for hydration, exercise, and math challenges. Notifications are a key feature of the extension.
  • Alarms: This allows the extension to schedule and deliver reminders at specific intervals, such as hourly hydration or math notifications.
  • Storage: This is used to store user preferences (like math difficulty levels) locally on your browser or synced across devices.

Data Sharing and Selling

Your privacy is a top priority. We strictly ensure that:

  • We do not sell or share your data with any third parties.
  • We do not transfer your data for purposes unrelated to the core functionality of this extension.
  • We do not use or process your data for lending, creditworthiness, or any financial purposes.

Third-Party Services

This extension does not rely on or interact with third-party services. All required resources and functionalities are implemented directly within the extension and run locally in your browser.

Updates

As we continue to improve this extension, we may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new features or changes in practices. If significant updates are made, we will notify users in the extension’s release notes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the extension, please contact us at: damper.dev@pm.me.
